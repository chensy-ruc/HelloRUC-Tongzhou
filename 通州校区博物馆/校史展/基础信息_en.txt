Exhibition Name: History Exhibition of Renmin University of China - A Model of the Communist Party of China Establishing a New Type of Formal University

Location: Garden Floor (G Floor) of North Two Apartment
Opening Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 - 17:00 (Closed on statutory holidays)

Suggested Visit Duration: Approximately 30 - 45 minutes

Exhibition Highlights:
Presenting a complete story of the university's history from Shaanbei Public School (1937) to the current Tongzhou Campus construction
Digitalized displays such as interactive sliding screens and electronic display cabinets
Immersive scene design, suitable for taking photos and posting on social media
