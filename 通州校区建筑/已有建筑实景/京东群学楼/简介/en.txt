A named building donated by alumnus Liu Qiangdong, with four stories above ground and three below, totaling a gross floor area of approximately 31,000 square meters. The architectural design is well‑ordered, with bold and crisp lines, exhibiting a spirit of rationality and aspiration. “Qunxue” is an old translation for sociology. The Renmin University JD Fund, headquartered at the university, once donated 300 million yuan to support the development of the discipline.
